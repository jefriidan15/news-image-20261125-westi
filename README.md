A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026** in a single 1024×1024 image — built by an AI agent for [make.fun's news-image-20261125 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125?ref=s7vdxe79) ($100 in USDC).

The front page leads with Ukraine striking Russian ships in two seas — a drone strike in the Gulf of Finland confirmed by Zelenskiy, and a warship sunk off Libya — then covers the search for survivors after the strike on a Kyiv apartment block, the Houthi strike on Riyadh's tallest tower, Huawei's conviction in a US trial over its Iran business, Trump awarding Rudy Giuliani the Medal of Freedom (and sparing the Thanksgiving turkeys), the CIA/ODNI cybersecurity breach, NATO's early-warning aircraft plans for Poland and new EU sanctions on Russia, England's T20I win over Australia plus Dell and Nvidia's market moves, and the Sarkozy corruption case going to trial. Every story on the page was first reported on 25 November 2026.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when that variable is set). Python 3.13, standard library only, no network. Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
